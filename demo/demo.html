Coral Bleaching Buildup — Lakshadweep

The Lakshadweep coral site scores 55/100 on bleaching risk, putting it at Alert Level 1 and indicating a significant chance of bleaching (high confidence).

Composite score 55/100 · Alert Level 1 — significant bleaching risk likely
What's driving the score

Composite = 60% Degree Heating Weeks (NOAA Coral Reef Watch-style) + 25% chlorophyll-a drift + 15% historical bleaching frequency. The frequency factor is illustrative (keyed to known global mass-bleaching years 2010/2016/2020, not a measured per-reef event log) — every other input is computed from recorded sensor history.
